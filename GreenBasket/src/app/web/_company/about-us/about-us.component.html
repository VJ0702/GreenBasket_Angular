Who We Are?

India's trusted destination for 100% organic, farm-fresh fruits and vegetables delivered straight to your doorstep.

Founded in 2020, GreenBasket was born from a simple yet powerful vision — to make healthy, organic food accessible to every Indian household. We partner directly with certified organic farmers across India, eliminating middlemen to bring you the freshest produce at fair prices while ensuring farmers receive their rightful earnings.

Our commitment goes beyond just selling organic products. We're building a sustainable ecosystem that supports local farming communities, promotes eco-friendly practices, and helps families make healthier food choices. Every product in our basket is carefully sourced, quality-checked, and delivered with care.

With over 50,000+ happy families trusting us for their daily grocery needs, we've grown to become Rajasthan's leading online organic marketplace. Our state-of-the-art cold chain ensures your fruits and vegetables reach you as fresh as they were picked from the farm.
