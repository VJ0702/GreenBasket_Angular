Welcome to GreenBasket

GreenBasket ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website www.greenbasket.com or use our mobile application. By using our services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected in accordance with Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable Indian laws.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including: Personal Information such as name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and payment details when you create an account or place an order. Transaction Information including products purchased, order history, and payment method. Device Information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers. Location Data to provide accurate delivery services and show nearby delivery options.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: Process and fulfill your orders including delivery of organic fruits, vegetables, and grocery products. Send order confirmations, delivery updates, and customer support communications. Personalize your experience by recommending products based on your preferences and purchase history. Improve our services through analytics and customer feedback. Prevent fraud and ensure secure transactions.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

GreenBasket uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Essential Cookies are necessary for the website to function properly, including cart and checkout functionality. Analytics Cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. Marketing Cookies are used to deliver relevant advertisements. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings, though disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with: Delivery Partners to fulfill your orders and provide real-time tracking. Payment Processors such as Razorpay, Paytm, and banks for secure payment processing. Service Providers who assist in analytics, customer support, and marketing. Legal Authorities when required by law or to protect our rights. All third parties are bound by confidentiality agreements and data protection standards.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information including: SSL Encryption for all data transmission. Secure Payment Gateway - we never store your complete card details. Access Controls limiting employee access to personal data. Regular Security Audits to identify and address vulnerabilities. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
